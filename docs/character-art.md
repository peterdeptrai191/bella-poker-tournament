# Bella occupational characters

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, with the user-supplied Bella flame mascot as the identity/style reference. Transparent backgrounds are preserved. Production sprites are 512px WebP files in `public/assets/characters/`.

Shared prompt: One full-body anthropomorphic Bella flame creature; confident grin, expressive white eyes, dark navy outlines, white cartoon gloves, navy sneakers. Polished esports mascot cel shading, front-facing slight three-quarter pose, readable at 100px. Square canvas, centered, transparent alpha background. No text, wordmark, border, watermark, chair, table or extra figures.

Individual prompt additions:

- `firefighter.webp`: Red/scarlet flame, red firefighter helmet and turnout jacket, gold reflective bands, fire hose nozzle.
- `doctor.webp`: Cyan/blue flame, white doctor coat, stethoscope, head mirror, medical clipboard.
- `engineer.webp`: Golden yellow flame, yellow hard hat, navy work overalls, wrench.
- `chef.webp`: Orange flame, white chef toque and jacket, orange apron, frying pan.
- `gardener.webp`: Emerald green flame, straw hat, green overalls, potted sprout and trowel.
- `dj.webp`: Purple/violet flame, headphones, purple jacket, vinyl record.

Characters are cosmetic only. Choices persist locally and on the authenticated room player, and are included in public room state so every participant sees the same character.
